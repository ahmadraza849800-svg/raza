6-Year Comprehensive Dars-e-Nizami (Alimah / Fazilah)

مکمل ۶ سالہ درسِ نظامی کورس (عالمہ و فاضلہ سند)

A prestigious six-year intensive scholarship in classical Islamic sciences. Structured to cultivate genuine expertise in the Arabic language, foundational and comparative Hanafi jurisprudence (Fiqh & Usul al-Fiqh), Quranic exegesis (Tafsir), Islamic logic (Mantiq), rhetoric (Balaghat), and the supreme Hadith canon (Daura-e-Hadith / Sihah Sitta). Instructed and mentored exclusively by certified female scholars (Alimat) with administrative guidance from Principal Sufi Jamshaid Alam and Vice Principal Allama Ahmad Raza Khan.

YEAR 1 (Al-Oula)

Arabic Grammar & Tajweed

Mizan-us-Sarf, Munsha'ib, Nahw Mir, Duroos-ul-Lughah, Tajweed, Seerat-un-Nabi ﷺ, Ta'leem-ul-Islam.

YEAR 2 (As-Sania)

Syntax & Foundational Fiqh

Ilm-us-Seegha, Hidayat-un-Nahw, Nur-ul-Idah, Mukhtasar-ul-Qudoori (Ibadat), Intro to Usul al-Shashi.

YEAR 3 (As-Salisa)

Advanced Nahw & Logic

Kafiyah, Mukhtasar-ul-Qudoori (Transactions & Mu'amalat), Isaghuji & Mirqat (Mantiq), Riaz-us-Saliheen.

YEAR 4 (Ar-Rabia)

Rhetoric & Hadith Principles

Sharh Wiqayah, Talkhees-ul-Miftah (Balaghat), Usul al-Shashi, Mishkat-ul-Masabih (Vol 1), Aqeeda Tahawiyya.

YEAR 5 (Al-Khamisa)

Hidayah & Tafseer al-Jalalayn

Al-Hidayah (Awwalain), Tafseer-ul-Jalalayn, Mishkat-ul-Masabih (Vol 2), Women's Fatawa, Comparative Jurisprudence.

YEAR 6 (Daura-e-Hadith)

Kutub al-Sittah Hadith Mastery

Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abi Dawud, Jami' al-Tirmidhi, Sunan an-Nasa'i, Sunan Ibn Majah, Muwatta.

Modern Academic Subjects

11th & 12th • Higher Secondary Certificate Duration: 2 Academic Years

F.A. (Faculty of Arts / Humanities)

A flexible higher-secondary certificate offering rich combinations of humanities and classical Islamic literature.

Compulsory Subjects:

English, Urdu, Islamic Education (Part 1), Pakistan Studies (Part 2), Tarjuma-tul-Quran-ul-Majeed.

Elective Combinations:

Islamic Studies (Elective), Arabic Language, Civics, Education, Home Economics, Sociology.

Concurrent Dars-e-Nizami Option:

Students may concurrently attend classes for the foundational years of the 6-Year Dars-e-Nizami program.

Eligibility: Passed Matriculation Inquire for F.A.
4-Year Degree • Higher Education Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)

B.S. Islamic Studies (۴ سالہ ڈگری پروگرام)

Rigorous 4-year undergraduate degree program aligned with Higher Education Commission guidelines, preparing women researchers, educators, and scholars.

Year 1 (Sem 1-2)

Quranic Sciences, Arabic Grammar, Islamic Worldview, Communication.

Year 2 (Sem 3-4)

Usul al-Fiqh, Islamic History, Philosophy of Law, Ilm al-Kalam.

Year 3 (Sem 5-6)

Hadith Textual Exegesis, Islamic Finance, Dawah Methodology, Research Methods.

Year 4 (Sem 7-8)

Comparative Religions, Contemporary Ethics, Research Thesis / Capstone.

Eligibility: F.A. / F.Sc / Sanad-e-Khasa Inquire for B.S. Program
