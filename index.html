Our Foundation & Sacred Mission

Dedicated Exclusively to the Dignity and Education of Women

Dar ul Uloom Rizvia Masoomia was established with a singular, noble objective: to provide female students with a sanctuary of sacred knowledge and contemporary enlightenment, free from compromise, surrounded by moral uprightness, modesty, and sincere spiritual guidance.

Harmonious Synthesis: Deen & Dunya

We believe that classical Islamic jurisprudence, Quranic sciences, and Prophetic Hadith harmonize beautifully with contemporary educational qualifications. At Dar ul Uloom Rizvia Masoomia, students do not have to choose between their faith and academic progression; they cultivate both simultaneously.

Uncompromised Modesty (Purdah) & Complete Safety

Parents and guardians place their supreme trust in our institution because of our steadfast adherence to Islamic boundaries. The campus is strictly designated for women, supervised by qualified female educators and senior scholars who instill modesty, grace, and spiritual poise.

Daily Tarbiyah & Character Building

Education is incomplete without internal purification (Tazkiyah). Through regular Adab sessions, recitation of the Holy Quran with Tajweed, Masnoon prayers, and Sunnah etiquette, we mentor young women into compassionate mothers, scholars, and pillars of society.

★ Flagship Sacred Curriculum • برائے طالبات

6-Year Comprehensive Dars-e-Nizami Program

درس نظامی برائے خواتین — ۶ سالہ عالمہ و فاضلہ کورس

The classical curriculum of Ahl-us-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah adapted with meticulous care exclusively for female scholars. Master the Arabic language, Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh), Quranic Tafsir, and the blessed Prophetic Traditions (Hadith) culminating in Daura-e-Hadith.

01 Al-Oula (سال اول)

Year 1: Foundation of Arabic & Grammar

Cultivating the core grammar foundation of the Arabic tongue and pristine Quranic pronunciation.

  • • Mizan-us-Sarf & Munsha'ib (Arabic Morphology)
  • • Nahw Mir (Arabic Syntax)
  • • Tajweed-ul-Quran & Nazra Mastery
  • • Duroos-ul-Lughat-il-Arabiyyah
  • • Basic Fiqh (Ta'leem-ul-Islam) & Seerat-un-Nabi ﷺ
Stage: Grammatical Fluency
02 As-Sania (سال دوم)

Year 2: Intermediate Syntax & Fiqh

Transition from basic rules to intermediate sentence structures and systematic Hanafi Fiqh for worship.

  • • Ilm-us-Seegha (Advanced Conjugation)
  • • Hidayat-un-Nahw (Intermediate Syntax)
  • • Nur-ul-Idah & Mukhtasar-ul-Qudoori (Ibadat)
  • • Al-Qira'at-ul-Wadiha (Prose & Comprehension)
  • • Introduction to Usul al-Shashi (Jurisprudence principles)
Stage: Applied Legal Literacy
03 As-Salisa (سال سوم)

Year 3: Advanced Texts & Logic (Mantiq)

Engaging classical texts in civil dealings (Mu'amalat) and foundational intellectual reasoning.

  • • Kafiyah (Mastery of Arabic Grammar)
  • • Mukhtasar-ul-Qudoori (Commercial & Social transactions)
  • • Isaghuji & Mirqat (Principles of Logic)
  • • Riaz-us-Saliheen (Hadith Ethics & Adab)
  • • Advanced Arabic Composition & Dialogue
Stage: Critical Comprehension
04 Ar-Rabia (سال چہارم)

Year 4: Balaghat & Mishkat al-Masabih

Eloquent Arabic rhetoric, theological tenets (Aqeeda), and comprehensive study of Prophetic sayings.

  • • Sharh Wiqayah (Advanced Jurisprudence)
  • • Talkhees-ul-Miftah (Arabic Rhetoric & Balaghat)
  • • Usool-ush-Shashi (In-depth Legal Theory)
  • • Mishkat-ul-Masabih (Volume 1)
  • • Aqeeda Tahawiyya & Sunni Creed
Stage: Scholarly Eloquence
05 Al-Khamisa (سال پنجم)

Year 5: Hidayah & Tafseer al-Jalalayn

Advanced comparative jurisprudence and comprehensive Quranic commentary with linguistic nuances.

  • • Al-Hidayah (Awwalain — The Pinnacle of Hanafi Law)
  • • Tafseer-ul-Jalalayn (Exegesis of the Quran)
  • • Mishkat-ul-Masabih (Volume 2)
  • • Contemporary Women's Fatawa & Modern Inquiries
  • • Islamic Inheritance Law (Ilm al-Fara'id)
Stage: Deep Doctrinal Mastery
06 Daura-e-Hadith (دورۂ حدیث شریف)

Year 6: Daura-e-Hadith (Kutub al-Sittah)

The supreme capstone year: exhaustive textual study of the six canonical Hadith collections with Sanad.

  • ★ Sahih al-Bukhari (الجامع الصحيح للبخاري)
  • ★ Sahih Muslim (صحيح مسلم)
  • ★ Sunan Abi Dawud & Jami' al-Tirmidhi
  • ★ Sunan an-Nasa'i & Sunan Ibn Majah
  • ★ Muwatta Imam Malik & Tahawi
Certification: Alimah / Fazilah Sanad-e-Faraghat
