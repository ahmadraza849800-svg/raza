About Dar ul Uloom Rizvia Masoomia

تعارف جامعہ دار العلوم رضویہ معصومیہ (برائے طالبات)

A legacy of sacred Islamic scholarship and contemporary academic pursuit, providing daughters of the Ummah with safety, dignity, and moral elevation.

Our Noble Mission

Nurturing Mothers, Scholars, and Leaders of Tomorrow

Women are the primary educators of human civilization. When a woman is endowed with authentic Islamic scholarship and high moral character, an entire generation is spiritually enlightened. Dar ul Uloom Rizvia Masoomia was established upon this realization.

Under the visionary stewardship of Principal Sufi Jamshaid Alam and Vice Principal Allama Ahmad Raza Khan, our institution blends the classical depth of the 6-Year Dars-e-Nizami with recognized board schooling in Middle, Matric, F.A., and degree programs like B.S. Islamic Studies.

100% Female Strictly segregated, guarded, and Purdah-compliant environment.
Classical & Modern Dual integration of classical Islamic texts and formal school degrees.

Our Core Principles:

  • ✔ Adherence to Ahl-us-Sunnah: Grounded in authentic Sunni traditions, love of the Holy Prophet ﷺ, and respect for the Companions and Awliya.
  • ✔ Uncompromising Modesty: Preserving Islamic veil and feminine dignity in every classroom and residential wing.
  • ✔ Female Faculty Mentorship: Senior educator Ayesha Ijaz and our dedicated team of Alimat directly instruct and nurture every student.
  • ✔ Financial Accessibility: Ensuring financial hardship never stands between a deserving female student and the knowledge of Deen.

The Campus Experience at Dar ul Uloom Rizvia Masoomia

Designed with intention to foster tranquility, focus, and spiritual development.

01

Spiritual Sanctuary & Prayer

Spacious prayer halls where students perform daily prayers with collective Sunnah Azkar, Durood Shareef recitations, and Quranic completion circles (Khatm-e-Quran).

02

Islamic Library & Research

Rich repository of classical Tafsir, Sihah Sitta Hadith commentaries, Hanafi Jurisprudence encyclopedias, Arabic lexicons, and contemporary academic literature.

03

Safe Residential Dormitories

Clean, wholesome, and guarded hostel facilities with hygienic dining, round-the-clock female warden supervision, and quiet study halls for outstation students.
