Our All-Women Instructional Faculty

At Dar ul Uloom Rizvia Masoomia, direct student instruction inside classrooms, labs, and prayer halls is conducted entirely by qualified female scholars (Alimat) and experienced female subject specialists. Our staff holds certifications from recognized boards and prestigious Wifaq institutions.

This segregation guarantees total comfort, privacy, and an uninhibited learning experience for every student, allowing them to raise questions, participate actively in debates, and grow in confidence.
